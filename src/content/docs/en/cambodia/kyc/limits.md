---
title: KYC Limits
description: Payin and payout limits before and after KYC approval in Cambodia
---

To increase a user's payin and payout transaction limits, the merchant can create a KYC application for the user. After the user completes KYC and is approved, the system automatically adjusts the user's payin and payout limits according to the table below.

| Currency | User Type    | Payin        | Payout        |
|----------|--------------|--------------|---------------|
| USD      | Non-KYC      | 0–20,000     | 0–50,000      |
| USD      | KYC Approved | Unlimited    | 0–50,000      |
| KHR      | Non-KYC      | 0–80,000,000 | 0–200,000,000 |
| KHR      | KYC Approved | Unlimited    | 0–200,000,000 |
